## Description

Two 2026 incidents lost time on ownership. In the double-booking incident it took 39
minutes to find the right team; in the 429 storm it took 18 minutes to page booking. Rewrite
the on-call policy so escalation is by owning team (CODEOWNERS, ADR-0009), with explicit
timers and incident roles.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Severity table with response targets for SEV1 to SEV4.
2. Escalation table mapping symptom areas to owning teams.
3. Explicit timers for when to page the owner (10 minutes SEV1, 20 minutes SEV2).
4. Postmortem expectations and template reference (OPS-44).
5. Published as markdown and PDF; PDF generated by `scripts/build_pdfs.py`.
6. Reviewed by all four service teams.
